**Eicher Motors Ltd. – Equity Research & Financial Modelling**

Project Overview:
Developed an equity research model for Eicher Motors Ltd. to analyse its financial performance, forecast future results and estimate its intrinsic value using different valuation methods.

Key Areas Covered:
- Historical Financial Statement Analysis
- Reformulated Income Statement
- Revenue and Profit Forecasting
- Asset and Debt Schedules
- Beta and WACC Calculation
- DDM, FCFF and FCFE Valuation
- Relative Valuation against Industry Peers

Key Findings:

1. **Revenue Growth:** Revenue from operations increased from ₹9,154 crore in FY20 to ₹23,408 crore in FY26.

2. **Future Growth:** Revenue is projected to reach approximately ₹57,914 crore by FY31 based on the model's assumptions.

3. **Profitability:** EBITDA is projected to increase from ₹5,785 crore in FY26 to ₹13,726 crore in FY31.

4. **Cost of Capital:** Calculated beta of approximately 0.87 and WACC of 9.60% using the CAPM approach.

5. **DDM Valuation:** The Dividend Discount Model estimates an intrinsic value of approximately ₹3,119 per share.

6. **Relative Valuation:** Peer-based valuation gives an average estimated value of approximately ₹5,981 per share using P/E, EV/EBITDA and EV/Revenue multiples.

Tools & Skills Used:
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial Modelling
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Financial Forecasting
- Beta and WACC
- DDM, DCF and FCFF/FCFE
- Relative Valuation

Conclusion:
The model shows strong projected revenue and profitability growth for Eicher Motors. Different valuation methods provide different estimates of the company's intrinsic value. The analysis helped in understanding financial forecasting, cost of capital and the use of valuation techniques in investment research.
